32/// The footprint of the control or display @p element, in units, before

39/// A layout that cannot be drawn: a group given a `size` smaller than what

50/// Lays out @p page, a tree whose root is a `page`, in place: each
51/// section's children take an `at` and a `span` (those given are kept),

53/// the one before stacks under it, or in rows of the section's `columns`;
54/// each section and pedal takes the `size` it needs, unless it gives one;
55/// panels, synth panels, and pedalboards are checked against a `size` they
